01 True / False
Nima was feeling energetic and happy because he did well on his math exam.

Look at Part 1 where Nima first answers Sara. What did he say about how he was feeling? And what did he say about the last question? Try again!

02 True / False
Ali is a new student from Shiraz who is described as friendly, talkative, and clever.
Reconsider this one:
Check Part 2. How many descriptions of Ali can you find? Look at what both Sara and Nima said about him. Try again!
Perfect!
Right! Sara described him as "friendly and talkative" from Shiraz, and Nima added "he's really clever too". You gathered all three descriptions!

Reza said: "Let's invite him to join us for lunch tomorrow." Sara agreed it would make Ali feel welcome. Great reading!

04 Multiple Choice
Why did Reza say, "I almost forgot!"?
Re-read Part 3:
Right before Reza says "I almost forgot!", what does Nima say he needs to do? Look at the line just before Reza's reaction. Try again!
Great job!
Exactly! Nima said "I need to study for the English test tomorrow", and Reza replied with "Oh, the English test is tomorrow? I almost forgot!" You followed the chain of events perfectly.
05 Short Answer
Why did Sara tell Nima not to worry about his math exam?
Sara told Nima not to worry because he is one of the most hard-working students in their class. She said she was sure that he did well on the exam.
06 Short Answer
How did the three friends describe Ali, and why did they want to invite him to lunch?
They described Ali as friendly, talkative, and clever. They wanted to invite him to lunch to make him feel welcome in his first week at school.
